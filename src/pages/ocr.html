What to expect

  • Clean printed text: near-perfect.
  • Faxes and photocopies: good, with occasional wrong characters.
  • Handwriting: usually comes back too uncertain to trust, and we name those pages rather than guess.
  • Tables keep their words, not their grid.

Here vs. the app

On this page OCR is unlimited and needs no account, one file at a time. The Android app has it too, as Read a scan: it gives you the text on screen, to copy or save as a .txt file.

Free here means all of it, one file at a time, with no account: reading a document of any length, as many documents as you like.
