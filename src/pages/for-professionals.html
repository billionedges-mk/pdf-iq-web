Run it on your own network

This is the part a firm buys and an individual never would. Every tool here is a static page that does its work in the browser tab — no server does any of it, which is why the site keeps working with its own server switched off. Your IT can host the whole thing internally, on a machine with no route to the internet at all.

Client documents then never touch a network that leaves the building, and that is a statement your IT can verify by unplugging something rather than one you have to take on trust. It is also the honest reason this is cheap for us to offer: the site is already built this way.

Redaction that removes rather than covers

Most tools draw a black rectangle over the words. The words stay in the file underneath it, and anyone who selects the area or opens the file in a text extractor gets them back. That has put real documents in the news. What a firm needs is the content gone from the file.

This one is not built, and it is the expensive item on this page. It is genuinely reachable from what already exists — the compressor decodes, rewrites and replaces the streams inside a PDF, which is the same class of work — but it is real engineering rather than a switch, and we would rather say so here than promise it and cost it afterwards.
