Receipts, IDs and signed pages.

Those are the usual reason people are here, and they are exactly the images you should not be handing to a server. This one builds the document in your browser instead.

Photos carry more than the picture.

A phone photo has GPS coordinates, the phone model and the capture time inside it by default. This page can strip all three before the image goes on a page.

About iPhone HEIC.

Safari can read HEIC; Chrome and Firefox cannot, and we will say so rather than fail vaguely. Sharing photos out of the Photos app usually converts them to JPEG on the way.
